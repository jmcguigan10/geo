# Autonomous-region scope and primary sources

Research checked: 6 October 2026. This is a source register for the expanded geography pool, not a claim that every form of local self-government worldwide has been mapped. The generated place catalogue records which of these candidates have playable geometry.

The pool covers countries, distinct overseas territories, and named internal autonomous or specially self-governing territorial regions. It includes special constitutional/statutory autonomy, autonomous republics and districts, devolved constituent regions, and internal self-governing territories. Ordinary federal states/provinces and ordinary municipal governments are outside this pool. Political powers vary substantially; the game does not equate autonomy with sovereignty.

Internal regions carry `parentId` metadata. The game never simultaneously selects an internal region and an ancestor country/region. Overseas dependencies represented independently without `parentId` can be selected separately.

### Russian republics and named autonomous subjects

The [Russian Ministry of Justice's constitutional text](https://minjust.gov.ru/ru/documents/8011/), articles 5 and 65, identifies the republics and named autonomous subjects. Its territorial claims in occupied Ukrainian regions are not treated as a neutral international boundary authority.

The 21 republics within Russia's internationally recognised territory are Adygea, Altai, Bashkortostan, Buryatia, Chechnya, Chuvashia, Dagestan, Ingushetia, Kabardino-Balkaria, Kalmykia, Karachay-Cherkessia, Karelia, Khakassia, Komi, Mari El, Mordovia, North Ossetia–Alania, Sakha/Yakutia, Tatarstan, Tuva/Tyva, and Udmurtia.

Also represented are the Jewish Autonomous Oblast and the 4 autonomous okrugs: Chukotka, Khanty-Mansi/Yugra, Nenets, and Yamalo-Nenets. Ordinary oblasts and krais are outside this expansion. Republic status and the named autonomous-subject statuses have different legal bases and should retain distinct metadata labels.

### Indonesia

Aceh, Yogyakarta, Jakarta, and the Papua special-autonomy area are supported candidates. The [Indonesian government's financing disclosure](https://api-djppr.kemenkeu.go.id/web/api/v1/media/E2956E5E-CE06-4635-84FF-832A4F45BFAB) identifies special status/autonomy for these areas. [Cabinet Secretariat: Papua special autonomy](https://setkab.go.id/pengakuan-hak-hak-adat-dalam-kebijakan-otonomi-khusus-papua-tantangan-dalam-implementasinya/) describes its legal basis and special governmental powers.

The Natural Earth snapshot has historical Papua and West Papua province polygons. Since 2022 the area has been divided into 6 provinces: Papua, West Papua, South Papua, Central Papua, Highland Papua, and Southwest Papua. The [Cabinet Secretariat's initial creation announcement](https://setkab.go.id/pembentukan-tiga-provinsi-baru-di-papua/) documents the first new provinces and their special-autonomy funding; its [December 2022 announcement](https://setkab.go.id/provinsi-papua-barat-daya-diresmikan-indonesia-sekarang-miliki-38-provinsi/) documents Southwest Papua. The combined historical footprint can serve as a clearly labelled **Papua special-autonomy area**, but it must not be described as accurate individual polygons for all 6 present-day provinces.

### Bangsamoro, Philippines

Bangsamoro/BARMM is included using the best available provincial aggregation. Current administrative status and historical map geometry must be distinguished.

The [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://psa.gov.ph/content/province-sulu-officially-transferred-region-ix-zamboanga-peninsula) confirms Sulu's exclusion from BARMM and its transfer to Region IX in July 2025. Sulu must therefore be omitted from the region's game footprint.

The available aggregation includes historical Maguindanao, Lanao del Sur, Basilan, Tawi-Tawi, and Cotabato City. Its known limitations are the omission of the Special Geographic Area's constituent barangays and the inclusion of Isabela City within the coarse Basilan polygon even though that city is outside BARMM. Historical Maguindanao also predates its split into Maguindanao del Norte and Maguindanao del Sur. Treat this as an approximate region boundary, not a current legal survey.

## Disputed and de facto entries

The user explicitly requested Abkhazia and South Ossetia. They are separate game entries with a disputed-status description. Other map-unit entries such as Transnistria, Somaliland, and Puntland also need their own status descriptions rather than being silently called sovereign countries.

[Georgia's Constitution](https://www.parliament.ge/en/legislation/constitution) identifies Abkhazia as an autonomous republic, while de facto administration differs. This source does not establish South Ossetia as a recognised independent state. The map's inclusion of either area is for identification gameplay.

Crimea has an autonomous-region history/status under Ukraine and is under Russian occupation; it must retain a disputed/occupied status note rather than being counted as an additional undisputed Russian republic. [UN General Assembly resolution 68/262](https://documents.un.org/access.nsf/get?DS=A%2FRES%2F68%2F262&Lang=E&OpenAgent=) affirms Ukraine's internationally recognised territory and rejects changing Crimea's status through the 2014 referendum. [Resolution ES-11/4](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3990673/files/A_RES_ES-11_4-ES.pdf) similarly rejects Russia's attempted annexation of further Ukrainian regions. Avoid adding claimed Donetsk/Luhansk republics merely because they appear in the Russian constitutional list.

## Known coverage gaps below the map's administrative level

These are real autonomous or self-administered territories whose specific boundaries are not supplied by the selected first-level map source. They are recorded here rather than substituted with entire ordinary states/provinces:

- **India:** The Sixth Schedule includes 10 autonomous district councils: Karbi Anglong, North Cachar Hills/Dima Hasao, Bodoland, Khasi Hills, Jaintia Hills, Garo Hills, Tripura Tribal Areas, Chakma, Mara, and Lai. [North Eastern Council: Sixth Schedule](https://necouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/uploadfiles/Schedule%20VI.pdf). These council boundaries differ from full state footprints. Further councils exist under state legislation, so these 10 are not an exhaustive list of all Indian autonomous councils.
- **China:** Autonomous prefectures and counties/banners exist below the 5 first-level autonomous regions. The [National Bureau of Statistics-hosted presentation](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/pdf/202010/P020201012524513990305.pdf), page 7, separately identifies the second and third levels; its counts are historical to the presentation date and should not be presented as a freshly verified 2026 total.
- **Myanmar:** Naga, Danu, Pa-O, Palaung, and Kokang self-administered zones, plus the Wa self-administered division. [Government Ministry of Information: administrative bodies](https://www.moi.gov.mm/moi%3Aeng/content/members-region-and-state-government-cabinet) identifies these 6 units. Natural Earth admin1 contains the surrounding states/regions, not each required subregion polygon. Constitutional designation and present de facto control can differ.
- **Panama:** Naso Tjër Di is newer than this Natural Earth snapshot; Guna de Madungandí and Guna de Wargandí are smaller comarcas absent as separate admin1 polygons. [Panamanian Judiciary: Naso Tjër Di, September 2026](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/unajug/noticias/organo-judicial-realiza-volanteo-y-sensibiliza-a-la-poblacion-durante-feria-comunitaria-en-comarca-naso-tier-di) confirms its separate current administration. Do not use all of Bocas del Toro as a substitute for Naso.
- **Bolivia:** Indigenous territorial autonomies, including Charagua Iyambae, Raqaypampa, Uru Chipaya, and Jatun Ayllu Yura, require municipal/territorial geometry. [State Autonomies Service: government register](https://fichas.sea.gob.bo/buscador/gaioc) and [Public Management School: overview](https://egpp.gob.bo/nota_prensa/fortalecimiento-de-las-autonomias-indigena-originario-campesinas-a-traves-de-la-formacion-en-gestion-publica/). This is a changing register, not just these 4 examples.
- **Indigenous self-government elsewhere:** First Nations, tribal governments, and other subnational treaty/self-government territories need a dedicated source and boundary audit. They should not be implied to be completely represented by a high-level global map.

## Geometry provenance and interpretation

The country/map-unit and admin1 boundaries come from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Country and region features may be aggregated or simplified for performance. Natural Earth's snapshot date and boundary conventions do not necessarily match the current administrative/legal situation.

The source links above justify inclusion and labels. They do not make the simplified game geometry an official border survey. Known differences belong in place metadata and the coverage information shown to players.
