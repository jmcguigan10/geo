      A LITTLE ABOUT MERIDIAN

      The whole world.
      A few missing pieces.

      A geography playground for people who like looking a little closer. Our first game takes the idea of JetPunk’s 20 missing countries quiz and opens the answer pool to small countries and separately administered territories.

      How it works

      Start a round, then name any of the 20 places that vanished. Correct answers return in green. Water and missing land share exactly the same blue; there are no borders, markers, or labels to give a missing place away. Common names and abbreviations work, and accents are optional.
